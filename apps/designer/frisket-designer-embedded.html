Two template designers in a host page

Each <frisket-designer> is a designer of its own, in a shadow root of its own: the first is handed a template through its report property, as a host hands over what its author composed last time; the second starts blank. Selecting, adding or loading in one does nothing to the other. Their accent comes from this page; their paper does not.

Both are told where the engine is - server="/", this page's own server - and keep its pages up to date as the template changes: beside the canvas for the first, which is where this page asks for them, and wherever the person chooses for the second. The choice is in the header, and kept.

The first is also handed this page's library through its subDocuments property: pieces the host keeps once - an article of law, the recipient's address block, the firm's legal notices - offered under Library in the palette and named st://reference in the template. They answer after a short delay, the way a backend would; the template keeps only their names.

The report also holds a chart, an SVG file its application drew. The canvas reads it by the engine's rules and draws what it read, so it is a picture only in name: its curves are marks and its labels text, in the PDF as here.
